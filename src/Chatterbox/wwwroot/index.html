Settings

Recommended defaults
Sets everything below to the best choice for this machine: the strongest engine your hardware runs fast, a 1.0 s update rate, and the standard pause behavior. Your microphone and auto-start players are untouched.
Recognition engine
Parakeet: best accuracy on CPU. Whisper: multilingual, GPU-accelerated with the CUDA pack.
Whisper model
Auto picks the best installed model for your hardware.
Chatbox update rate
How often new words reach the in-game window. 1.0 s is the floor — faster gets rate-limited by VRChat.
Typing indicator
Shows the chatbox "…" bubble while you're speaking.
Recognize player names Experimental
Nudges the engines toward the display names of players in your instance and fixes near-miss spellings. Parakeet uses beam search (a little more CPU); engine biasing applies at the next Start. Off = zero overhead.
New line after a pause
Pausing this long starts your next sentence on a new chatbox line.
Clear after quiet
After this much silence the window resets, so faded text never reappears. VRChat hides the bubble at about 30 s.

Desktop

Add to app grid
Copies Chatterbox into your home folder (~/.local/share/Chatterbox/app), adds it to your app grid, and shows the line for VRChat's Steam launch options. No root needed. Run it again after downloading a new version.

Speed check

How fast is recognition on this PC?
Times every installed engine on a bundled 14-second clip and rates each for live captioning. Takes under a minute; captions must be stopped. The result is also written to bench.log for bug reports.

Updates

Check for updates
Looks up the latest release on GitHub. One small request; nothing about you is sent.
Check when Chatterbox starts
Tells you at startup when a newer version exists — nothing installs by itself. Off: Chatterbox never contacts GitHub unless you press Check.
