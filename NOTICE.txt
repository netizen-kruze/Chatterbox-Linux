Chatterbox — live local speech-to-text captions for the VRChat chatbox
====================================================================

- Whisper.net and whisper.cpp — MIT (Sandro Hanea; Georgi Gerganov et al.)
- sherpa-onnx — Apache-2.0 (k2-fsa)
- ONNX Runtime — MIT (Microsoft)
- Photino.NET — Apache-2.0 (TryPhotino)
- Newtonsoft.Json — MIT (James Newton-King)
- LLamaSharp and llama.cpp — MIT (Martin Evans and contributors; Georgi

System components used at runtime, provided by the distribution and not
bundled: GTK 3, libnotify and WebKitGTK (the window), PipeWire's pw-record
or PulseAudio's parec (microphone capture), coredumpctl/journalctl (crash
records), the NVIDIA driver's libcuda.so.1 for optional GPU acceleration,
and the Vulkan loader (libvulkan.so.1) with the GPU driver's Vulkan ICD for
optional GPU-accelerated translation.
